[Fantasia · Park] Fairy-Tale Kingdom | Infinite Forms, Castle Redux

Design Highlights: Infinite Forms · Shape at Will · Classic Revival · Worlds Made Real

This project takes an innovative polymer composite as its core medium and, through a patented thermoplastic process combined with digital modeling, pushes beyond the expressive limits of traditional materials. The material’s extraordinary plasticity allows for the precise recreation of Renaissance-style classical reliefs as well as the flawless rendering of fantastical, future-fairy-tale curves. From Edenic gardens to interstellar castles, every vision can be brought within reach by the artisan’s hand.

This is not only a redefinition of the children’s theme-park typology, but a revolutionary interpretation of construction technology. Certified to international safety standards, the material is eco-friendly and non-toxic, weather-resistant, and impact-durable—ensuring a fantasy world that endures. As children roam between themed realms, they witness not only the magic of fairy tales, but a living showcase of craftsmanship and innovative spirit.
